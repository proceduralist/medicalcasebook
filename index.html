What's inside

Built the way you actually learn

A question comes up at the workstation. You write it down, find the answer later, cite what you found, and make it stick — without ever leaving one app.

01

Write it down in seconds

Get the topic down the moment it comes up, before it evaporates. Sorting it out properly can wait until you have time to do it justice.

02

Groups, and an A–Z index

Topics nest under groups you name yourself. The Mac opens them as a proper index — one you can scan, search, and jump through by letter.

03

Tag it four ways

Specialty, Anatomy, Disease and Kind, so a topic can be found from any direction. The tag list is yours to shape as your interests change.

04

Sources, properly cited

Add a source by DOI, by link, or by scanning a book. Drop numbered citations into your note and they keep themselves in order as you write.

05

Notes written in sections

Answer the topic as named sections in a table of contents, so a long note is still something you can navigate a year from now.

06

Study Mode

Hide the phrases you want to remember, then reveal them one at a time. Your own notes become the quiz — no second app, no flashcards to make.

07

Images kept with the topic

Keep the images that make the point alongside the topic they belong to, mark up what matters, and star the best ones into one browsable collection.

08

Export and share

Any topic prints as a clean, typeset document with its sources listed at the end — ready to send on, archive, or read on paper.

One casebook, two jobs

Write at the desk. Review anywhere.

The split is deliberate. Working an answer out — sections, citations, marking up images — belongs on a big screen with a keyboard. Going back over it later does not.
