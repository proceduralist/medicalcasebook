Frequently asked questions

How do I capture a new topic?

Open Capture from the sidebar, type the topic, and save. If you have written down something similar before, the app says so before you save, so you end up with one topic rather than two.

Every topic starts as Incomplete. Move it to Ongoing or Answered from its own page as you work through it.

What is the difference between a topic and a group?

A topic is one thing you are working out — a question, a concept, a technique. A group is a folder of related topics that you name yourself.

On the Mac, the Topics page is an A–Z index: groups and ungrouped topics sit together alphabetically, a letter strip jumps you down the list, and clicking a group opens it in the panel alongside. A topic does not need a group — it only joins one when you put it there.

How does tagging work?

Every topic can carry tags in four dimensions: Specialty, Anatomy, Disease and Kind. Open a topic, go to Tags, and add one. You can search the existing tags, create a new one by typing a name, and rename or delete any tag from the picker — right-click on Mac, long-press on iPhone.

Renaming a tag updates every topic that uses it.

How do I add sources and citations?

In a topic's Sources section, add a DOI or a link — or scan a book's barcode with the camera on iPhone and iPad. Citation details are fetched for you where they are available.

While writing on the Mac, use the cite button next to any source to drop a numbered citation into your note at the cursor. Citations renumber themselves as you add and reorder, and tapping one jumps to the source it points at.

How do notes and sections work?

Each topic has one note, written on the Mac as a set of named sections listed beside it. Add, rename, reorder and delete sections as the answer takes shape; the section list doubles as a table of contents for getting back to the part you want.

On iPhone and iPad the same note opens as an outline you can read section by section. That is what the phone is for — reviewing what you worked out, between cases or on the way home — rather than sitting down to write.

What is Study Mode?

Select a phrase in your note on the Mac and mark it to hide. With Study Mode on, every marked phrase is covered; click one to reveal it, and the section list shows how much is still hidden.

Turning Study Mode off restores everything. Hiding a phrase only affects how the note is displayed — nothing is removed from what you wrote, and exports are unaffected.

What is the Case Repository?

The repository collects your starred images from across every topic. Star an image and it appears there, carrying its topic's tags so you can filter by specialty.

To take an image out of the repository, tap its star again — either on the topic or on the repository tile. Removing it never deletes the image; it stays with the topic it came from.

How do I export a topic to PDF?

Open the topic and choose Share. The PDF includes the title, tags, note with its citations, numbered sources, and the topic's images, laid out as a document you can send on, archive or print.

My content isn't syncing between devices.
  • 01Make sure both devices are signed in to the same Apple Account.
  • 02Check Settings > Apple Account > iCloud and confirm iCloud Drive and Medical Casebook are enabled.
  • 03Confirm both devices have a network connection — sync pauses offline and catches up automatically.
  • 04Low iCloud storage can stall sync; check your available space.
  • 05If it still won't sync, restart both devices, then email us.
Is my content backed up? What if I delete the app?

With iCloud sync on, your content lives in your private iCloud database and is restored automatically when you reinstall the app on any device signed in to the same Apple Account.

Without iCloud, your content exists only on that device and is included in normal device backups — deleting the app deletes its local content, so we recommend keeping iCloud sync on.

I deleted something by accident.

Deleted topics, notes and images go to Trash rather than disappearing. Open Trash on either platform to read what is in there and put it back.

Does the app collect any of my data?

No. There are no servers, accounts, analytics or tracking. Everything stays on your device and in your own private iCloud. See the privacy policy for the details.
